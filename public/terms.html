Deep Social — Terms of Use (Beta)

Last updated: July 2026 · Version française ci-dessous · The English version prevails. Canonical text: TERMS.md

Deep Social is a hobby project in beta testing — a map-forward app for finding people and meetups nearby. These terms explain, in plain language, the deal between you and the project author. By signing in you agree to them. See also the Privacy Notice.

What the service is

  • Free, provided as-is, for testing. No company behind it, no paid tier.
  • A work in progress: features may break, change, or disappear, and the database may be reset at any time. Don't rely on it for anything important.

Who can use it

  • You must be 16 or older. The app asks you to confirm this before sign-in and records the confirmation on your profile. Deep Social is not directed at children, and under-16s may not use it (in Luxembourg, processing a younger person's data would also require parental consent — which this service does not support).
  • You sign in with a Google or Apple account, so you must meet that provider's own age and eligibility rules.

Your content and how you may use the app

  • You own the content you create — pins, photos, messages, profile details.
  • By posting, you grant the service the right to store and display it to other users as the app is designed to. Nothing more.
  • You agree not to post or do: anything illegal or content you have no right to share; harassment, threats, hate or bullying; sexual content involving minors — ever, in any form; spam or scams; impersonation; posting other people's personal data without consent.
  • Location and event pins must not put anyone in danger.

Moderation, reporting, and your rights in that process

  • You can report anything: pins and photos (⚑ on the event card), people (Report on their card or profile), and conversations (⚑ in the chat header). The reported person is not told who reported them.
  • Reports are reviewed and acted on within 24 hours. Confirmed violations are removed; the responsible account faces a warning, content removal, suspension, or termination depending on severity.
  • If your content or account is actioned, you will be told what and why (a statement of reasons).
  • If you think we got it wrong, reply through the contact channel — a human re-reviews. You always also have the right to complain to the Luxembourg Digital Services Coordinator or to a court.

Ending your use

  • Delete your account anytime: profile menu → Delete account (one cascade — see the Privacy Notice).
  • We may suspend or terminate access for breaking these terms or endangering others.

Safety features are not a safety guarantee

Guardian mode (and any other safety-flavoured feature) is a convenience tool, not a safety service or guarantee. It relies on your phone's battery, signal, and permissions and on third-party networks; its messages, location updates, and check-in alerts may be delayed, incomplete, or fail entirely. Do not rely on it in an emergency or as a substitute for contacting the emergency services (112 in the EU) or a trusted person. The project author does not monitor your meetups and is not responsible for your safety.

No warranty, limits on liability

The service comes with no warranty. To the extent the law allows, the project author is not liable for loss or harm arising from use. You are responsible for how you interact with people you meet through it — online and in person.

Changes & contact

Terms may change as the app evolves (see the date above). Questions, reports, requests: github.com/MagicBundle/deep-social/issues.
